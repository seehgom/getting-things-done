## Workflow preferences (from the repo owner)

- Merge pull requests as soon as they're ready (draft → ready for review
  → merge) without stopping to ask first. Don't wait for explicit
  "go ahead and merge" each time — that's the default now. Only hold off
  if the owner explicitly says to wait on a particular PR, or if there's
  a real blocker (failing CI, a merge conflict, something that needs
  their judgment call).
